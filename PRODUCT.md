# Product: Ideal Pools, LLC website

## Platform
- Platform: web
- Stack: static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (no frameworks, no tracking), hosted on GitHub Pages

## Users
- Pool owners (above-ground and in-ground) who need their pool closed for winter, often late in the season. Source: Facebook group post in "Ocean county small businesses and services." (2026-10-19).
- They arrive on a phone, from a Facebook post or Messenger link, inside Facebook's in-app browser. (Inferred from how the business advertises.)
- Secondary: the owner of Ideal Pools, who receives the site for free and must recognise their own business in it.

## Purpose
- Turn "LAST-MINUTE POOL CLOSINGS!" demand into booked closings without everything living in Facebook DMs.
- Give the business a findable home of its own (it has none; web search for its name returns competitors only).

## Positioning
- New company (2026) run by people with 10+ years of professional pool experience ("New company 2026!", "Over 10 years professional experience!", logo badge).
- "Reliable and affordable!" (caption).
- Logo: "We specialize in building above ground pools" · "We service all types of pools".

## Capabilities (services, verbatim from caption and logo badge)
- Above-ground & in-ground pool closings; professional winterization ("Last-minute pool closings").
- Opening · Closing · Cleaning
- Liner replacement · Equipment repairs · Acid washes · Leak detection · Deck coatings · & more
- Building above-ground pools ("We specialize in building above ground pools")
- Pool types: above ground, in-ground, fiberglass, vinyl liner

## Constraints
- Phone (848) 480-3729. Whether it takes texts is unknown. Preferred channel stated: Facebook Messenger ("Message us today to ask about availability and schedule your pool closing!").
- No email, no hours, no owner name, no service towns, no license/insurance info, no prices, no reviews, no real work photos.
- Service area must not be inferred (the ad was posted in an Ocean County NJ group; no town is stated).
- Do not merge with any other "Ideal Pools"-named company.

## Brand commitments
- Logo badge: royal and sky blue, deep navy band, a yellow sun, white lettering, a crested wave. Flyer adds frost white and snowflakes for the closing season.
- Tone: urgent and friendly ("Don't Wait Until It's Too Late!", "Keep Your Pool Safe This Winter!").
- Voice: "we / our" (an LLC; captions say "Message us").

## Evidence on hand
- Facebook profile image (logo badge, 720px) and the closing-season flyer (600px). Both are promotional graphics, not photos of real jobs.

## Principles
- Phone first; the first phone screen shows the offer and the way to book.
- Only facts from the brief, caption, logo and flyer; gaps go to HANDOFF.md, not onto the page.
